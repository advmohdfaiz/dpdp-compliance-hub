## 🏛️ Executive Summary

The **DPDP Compliance Hub** is an institutional-grade, client-side compliance management suite engineered for Indian and global **Data Fiduciaries** and **Significant Data Fiduciaries (SDFs)** under the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**.

Built with an ultra-minimalist, high-density enterprise architecture (*Inter* + *JetBrains Mono*), it provides data privacy officers (DPOs), general counsel, and compliance teams with an end-to-end statutory operating system.

---

## ⚡ Core Operational Modules

### 1. Statutory Workspace & Entity Setup
- **Fiduciary Parameter Configuration (Section 2(i)):** Dynamically configures legal entity identifiers, designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) particulars, physical registered offices, and customer support channels.
- **Dynamic Field Injection:** All 12 statutory policies, agreements, and regulatory filings auto-populate dynamically from these entity parameters.

### 2. Data Inventory & Lawful Processing Mapping
- **Personal Data Categorization:** Record specific data intake items, data principal categories, and internal access levels.
- **Bare-Act Lawful Grounds:** Map processing purposes strictly to statutory grounds:
  - **Section 6:** Free, informed, specific, unconditional, and unambiguous consent with clear affirmative action.
  - **Section 7:** Specified legitimate uses (voluntary provision s.7(a), State subsidies s.7(b), legal compliance s.7(c), medical emergency s.7(d), employment purposes s.7(i)).

### 3. 21-Step Bare-Act Statutory Roadmap
- **Structured Compliance Audit:** 21 actionable statutory obligations across Governance, Notice, Consent, Security Safeguards (s.8(5)), Processor Due Diligence (s.8(2)), Children's Data Safeguards (s.9), and Data Protection Board readiness.
- **Audit Logging:** Check off completed statutory milestones with live progress bar and timestamped audit notes.

### 4. 12 Statutory Legal Documents Factory (.docx Export)
Instant client-side document generation with real-time browser preview, markdown copying, print styling, and one-click **Microsoft Word (.docx)** export:
1. **Privacy Notice** *(Section 5 & Rules 3, 9, 14)*
2. **Website, Form & Counter Micro-Notices** *(Rules 3(c), 9, 14(1))*
3. **Standalone Consent Request** *(Section 6(1) & Rule 4)*
4. **Online Consent & Checkbox Specs** *(Section 6(1) & Rule 4)*
5. **Notice of Right of Withdrawal** *(Section 6(4) & Section 6(6))*
6. **Notice to Existing Users** *(Section 5(2))*
7. **CCTV / Physical Surveillance Notice** *(Section 5 & Section 7(a))*
8. **Verifiable Parental Consent Framework** *(Section 9 & Rule 11)*
9. **Vernacular Privacy Notice** *(Eighth Schedule / Section 5(3))*
10. **Data Principal Rights Intake Form** *(Sections 11–14 & Rule 14)*
11. **Statutory Response Templates (Access, Correction, Grievance)** *(Sections 11–13 & Rule 14)*
12. **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** *(Section 8(2) & Rule 7)*

### 5. Statutory Registers & Auditing
- **Consent Register (Section 6(10)):** Discharge statutory burden of proof before the Data Protection Board with notice version logs, withdrawal methods, and principal IDs.
- **Data Subject Rights Register (Sections 11–14 & Rule 14):** Log and track Access (s.11), Correction/Erasure (s.12), Grievance (s.13), and Nomination (s.14) requests within the statutory SLA window.
- **Data Processor Register (Section 8(2)):** Track third-party processor security safeguards, valid contracts, and audit status.
- **Compliance Maintenance Calendar:** Recurring 30, 90, 180, and 365-day automated statutory review reminders.

### 6. Incident Triage & Enforcement Modules
- **Section 8(6) Breach Incident Response Clock:** Immediate incident triage with live intimation countdown, containment checklists, and statutory notification generation for both the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) and affected Data Principals.
- **Section 33 & The Schedule Penalty Calculator:** Accurate calculation of statutory exposure under **The Schedule [See Section 33(1)]**:
  - Failure to prevent breach under s.8(5): Up to **₹250 Crore**
  - Failure to intimate Board/Principals under s.8(6): Up to **₹200 Crore**
  - Statutory quantum determination factors under s.33(2)(a)-(g).

## 🔒 Security & Privacy Architecture

- **100% Client-Side:** Operates entirely within the user's browser. No data, personal particulars, or documents are transmitted to external servers.
- **Local Persistence:** Business configuration and registers persist securely in browser .
- **Encrypted Backup & Portability:** One-click JSON database export and restore functionality.
